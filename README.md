One original one-line joke about office life, for a $5 bounty on make.fun. A judge model compares the jokes two at a time and the funniest lines take the top 10 places.

## The joke

> Someone stole my lunch from the office fridge, so I relabelled it 'Q3 budget review' and it survived the whole week.

116 characters. One original line: a petty office crime, a corporate-jargon weapon, and a punchline that lands where nobody expected.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, then reads it back and verifies the gate: UTF-8 text, 1 to 199 characters, exactly one line, byte-identical to the chosen joke.

Run: `node main.ts` (with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; falls back to `./output`).
